About us

People who love pets, looking after yours.

Everything Pet started as a neighborhood shelter and grew into a full care service: overnight boarding, daily walks, and a free adoption program.

Our promise

Every animal in our care gets a clean bed, fresh water, outdoor time, and staff who actually enjoy the job. Whether you need a night of boarding or a 30-minute walk, we treat your pet like one of our own.
